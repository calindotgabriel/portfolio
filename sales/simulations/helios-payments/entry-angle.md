## Primary angle

### Regulated-fintech authentication before partner onboarding

Position Calin as a senior Node.js/TypeScript engineer who has already strengthened authentication across service boundaries in an AWS-based, bank-facing regulated platform and can bring the same risk-aware delivery discipline to Helios's stated authentication work.

The credible promise is not that Calin already knows Helios's solution. It is that he has worked where inter-service trust, testing, safe change, and clear risk communication are part of shipping backend software that banks rely on.
