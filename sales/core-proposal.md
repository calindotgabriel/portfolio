## Ideal Customer Profile

Remote-EU product and platform teams that need a senior Node.js/TypeScript engineer for regulated fintech, payments, custody, DACH product delivery, migrations, performance, APIs, integrations, testing, or data-heavy backend work.

## Core Promise

Calin helps product teams reduce backend delivery risk in systems where correctness, performance, integration quality, and safe change matter. He can own meaningful slices of an existing system, communicate risks clearly, and ship within established quality standards.

## Primary Proof

- Bitpanda: built backend features for an institutional crypto-custody platform for banks using Fastify, GraphQL, TypeScript, and AWS; owned the crypto address-book backend end to end; strengthened authentication and introduced testing patterns adopted by the team.
- RWE: supported a Java-to-NestJS migration, profiled MongoDB-heavy paths, reduced exports from 45 to 12 minutes with worker pools, and improved API response times by 65%.
- ImmoScout24 Austria: shipped React/Node, search, listing, SEO, and Iterable work inside a mature tested distributed platform.
- Endava: delivered payments, publishing, and enterprise product work and mentored two junior developers.

## Segment Variants

Senior product engineer already proven with Austrian and German teams at Bitpanda, ImmoScout24, and RWE; EU citizen, Romania-based, remote-ready, and able to become useful inside established standards without a long runway.

Senior Node.js/TypeScript engineer for migrations and performance-sensitive APIs, with measured proof in Java-to-NestJS modernization, MongoDB profiling, worker pools, 45-to-12-minute exports, and 65% faster APIs.

## Commercial Guardrails

- Engagement: long-term remote-EU B2B; contract-to-hire only when the scope is strong.
- Availability: immediate.
- Anchor: €55–65/hour B2B for qualified work.
- Hard floor: €40/hour B2B.
- Never trade below the floor; negotiate scope, duration, weekly commitment, notice, payment terms, or other conditions instead.
