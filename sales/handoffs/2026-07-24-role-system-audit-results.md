## Recent Workflow Mapping

### Job-board profile setup

- **Owner:** `strengthen-contract-proof`.
- **Deliverable:** A draft-only profile pack containing professional summary, target title, skills, work-history checks, visibility/privacy checks, verified assets, and claims that must not be added.
- **Boundary:** The proof agent recommends wording and reviews consistency. Calin uploads the CV, connects accounts, accepts terms, changes privacy settings, and publishes edits.
- **Why this is not a new role:** Profile setup is proof packaging and discoverability work, already named in the skill's CV/LinkedIn recommendation scope. It is not a separate funnel stage.

### Application-form preparation

- **Owner:** `write-contract-outreach`.
- **Deliverable:** A field-by-field draft pack covering cover letter, short answers, rate response when requested, proof checklist, and explicit unknowns.
- **Supporting owner:** `strengthen-contract-proof` supplies approved profile/CV assets; `lead-contract-sales` confirms the account, motion, and duplicate state.
- **Boundary:** Calin enters personal data, reviews terms, uploads files, checks consent, and submits.
- **Why this is not a new role:** The writer already owns application notes and channel-specific contract outreach. A form is another outbound channel, not a distinct specialty.

### Application tracking

- **Owner:** `lead-contract-sales`.
- **Deliverable:** A canonical pipeline/account update based only on Calin's confirmation or a verified external event, including last touch, response state, duplicate control, next action, and checkpoint date.
- **Boundary:** No specialist may infer that an application was submitted from the existence of a draft or an open form.
- **Why this is not a new role:** Tracking, status, ownership, duplicates, metrics, and next actions are the core coordination responsibilities of the lead.

### Post-application checkpoints

- **Owner:** `lead-contract-sales` until a real reply.
- **Supporting owner:** `write-contract-outreach` prepares a conditional no-reply follow-up; `close-contract-opportunity` takes over reply, interview, qualification, and negotiation preparation.
- **Boundary:** Calin checks private inboxes/platforms and personally sends any approved follow-up. The lead must cancel no-reply motion when a reply arrives or the role closes.
- **Why this is not a new role:** The trigger is a pipeline date, and the work cleanly divides between existing coordination, drafting, and closing roles.

## Overlaps and Routing Rules

1. **Scout versus strategist:** The scout records a provisional likely angle only to explain fit. The strategist owns the selected account angle after lead activation.
2. **Strategist versus writer:** The strategist decides what to say and why; the writer decides how to express the approved angle in the selected channel. The writer must not invent a new pain point or stakeholder.
3. **Proof agent versus writer:** The proof agent improves reusable positioning and assets; the writer adapts already verified proof to one approved application or recipient. A one-account cover letter belongs to the writer, not the proof agent.
4. **Lead versus writer after application:** The lead owns checkpoint timing, listing/reply-state review, and duplicate prevention. The writer only supplies conditional drafts.
5. **Writer versus closer after reply:** A real response transfers conversation preparation to the closer. The closer may draft the next-step message as part of opportunity advancement; the writer should not run a parallel sequence.
6. **Scout versus closer on economics:** The scout records published rate evidence and rejects confirmed below-floor work. The closer qualifies unpublished or ambiguous economics after buyer engagement and prepares negotiation posture.
7. **Lead versus every specialist:** The lead owns status, priority, assignment, and canonical coordination. Specialists own bounded artifacts and return ownership; they do not independently advance an account.

These clarifications preserve the current ownership model. They do not change authority: agents draft and recommend; Calin performs every external action.

## Threshold for Reconsidering a New Role

Do not add a role now. Reassess only if at least one of these repeated conditions appears across multiple weeks or accounts:

- Five or more concurrent applications require recurring portal, document-version, consent, status, and checkpoint coordination that materially displaces lead prioritization.
- Application operations repeatedly produce missed deadlines, wrong attachments, duplicate submissions, or untracked portal messages despite the lead checklist.
- A stable, distinct deliverable emerges that cannot be cleanly split into reusable proof, channel copy, pipeline coordination, or closing preparation.

If that threshold is later met, the minimum candidate would be:

- **Name:** `operate-contract-applications`
- **Role:** Application operations coordinator
- **Trigger:** Lead-approved account is ready for an application or has an outstanding portal checkpoint.
- **Goal:** Prepare a complete, truthful, duplicate-safe application checklist and maintain the internal portal-status record.
- **Boundaries:** Never log in, accept terms, upload, submit, message, schedule, or change public settings; never create positioning or negotiate rates.
- **Inputs:** Approved account angle, verified CV/profile assets, application fields supplied by Calin, pipeline state, and channel deadline.
- **Outputs:** Field map, attachment/version checklist, unresolved questions, pre-submit review, and post-submission checkpoint handoff.
- **Why existing roles would no longer suffice:** Only justified if the recurring operational volume becomes large enough that splitting preparation among writer, proof agent, and lead causes measurable execution failures.

This is a contingency definition, not a recommendation to create the skill.
