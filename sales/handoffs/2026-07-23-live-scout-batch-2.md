## Qualification Gates

A candidate may be returned as qualified and considered for canonical activation only when all non-commercial hard gates are evidenced:

- **Current:** A publication/update date or another dated direct-source freshness signal shows the opportunity is current. Record both publication/update date and observation date. Evidence older than 45 days is stale unless a dated source directly confirms the opening remains active. Page visibility alone is not sufficient freshness evidence.
- **Contract form:** The source explicitly supports B2B, freelance, contractor, or fixed-term external engagement. Do not infer contract flexibility from a permanent role.
- **Technical fit:** Node.js and/or TypeScript is central to the work, not incidental.
- **English-speaking:** English is an evidenced working or application language and no mandatory language requirement conflicts with Calin's verified Romanian/English profile. An English-language webpage alone is not sufficient proof when working-language requirements are absent.
- **Remote-EU/Romania eligibility:** The source explicitly permits Romania, EU/EEA-wide remote contracting, or another clearly applicable cross-border European scope. Generic `remote`, a city label, or `Europe` without engagement-country support is insufficient for activation.
- **Reachable path:** A direct application URL, named recruiter, named public role owner, or reproducible public recruiter/contact route exists. Do not invent contact details.
- **Commercial floor:** Confirmed economics must not normalize below €40/hour B2B. If workload or billable hours are unstated, do not force a day/month-to-hour conversion. Unknown economics may receive zero rate-viability points and remain explicitly unresolved, but any published or confirmed below-floor opportunity must be rejected and never activated.

A score of 70 or more does not override a failed hard gate. Put near matches with one resolvable unknown in `Watchlist`; put stale, closed, permanent-only, incompatible-language/geography, below-floor, or materially weak records in `Rejected`.
