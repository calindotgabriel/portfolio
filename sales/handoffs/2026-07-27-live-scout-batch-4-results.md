## Controlling gates

An opportunity could activate only if all of the following were evidenced:

1. Current dated hiring or contract signal.
2. External B2B/freelance contract form.
3. Node.js/TypeScript backend or backend-heavy full-stack fit without an unsupported mandatory specialization.
4. English-working environment.
5. Romania or EU contracting eligibility.
6. Reachable, direct response or application path.
7. Economics not confirmed below the €40/hour B2B floor.
8. Score of at least 70/100.

`Remote`, `Europe`, `B2B`, and an unpublished rate were not inferred from one another. An active page without a publication/update/deadline signal received reduced currentness credit. A numerical score above 70 did not override a failed hard gate.
