# Market-Fit Plan: Land a Top Remote-EU Contract in 30 Days

Companion to `interview-narrative.md`. That doc is what to say in the room; this one is how to get in the room.

## The One Thing Being Iterated

**The Bitpanda credential is the spearhead.** "Built the backend of an institutional crypto-custody platform for banks" is the strongest, freshest, most differentiated proof in the profile — and the fintech/regulated market pays the top EU contract rates. Every asset (site headline, CV summary, case study, LinkedIn, outreach messages) now leads with it. Energy (RWE) and marketplace (Scout24) proof stay as secondary evidence, not headline.

## Target Segments (Priority Order)

1. **EU fintech / crypto / payments** — custody, banking infrastructure, PSPs, exchanges. Direct Bitpanda proof. Highest rates, most remote-friendly, actively filters for regulated-environment experience.
2. **DACH product platforms** — three consecutive AT/DE employers (Bitpanda, ImmoScout24, RWE) make "proven with Austrian and German teams, EU citizen, Bucharest timezone" a concrete de-risking pitch. This is the geographic wedge.
3. **Data-heavy platforms** (energy, marketplaces, publishing) — RWE / Scout24 / Endava proof as fallback breadth.

## Lead Channels (Where The 30 Qualified Leads Come From)

- **LinkedIn — highest ROI, do first:**
  - Headline: "Senior Backend Engineer · Node/TS · built institutional crypto-custody for banks at Bitpanda · available for EU B2B contracts".
  - Featured section: link the Bitpanda and RWE case studies + `/cv.pdf`.
  - Open-to-Work for contract roles.
  - **Request recommendations now** from Bitpanda / Scout24 / RWE colleagues — third-party proof is the single biggest missing sell factor in the whole profile.
- **DACH contract recruiters:** Darwin Recruitment, Hays, Computer Futures, Austin Fraser, and similar. 5–10 targeted recruiter intros beat 50 cold applications for B2B contracts.
- **Direct outbound to fintechs:** shortlist ~20 EU fintech/custody/payments companies hiring Node/TS. Short note, one concrete hook, link to the Bitpanda case study URL.
- **Boards:** cord, Welcome to the Jungle, RemoteOK, WeWorkRemotely; freelancermap and GULP specifically for the DACH contract market.
- **Warm network:** one message to each ex-colleague from Bitpanda, Scout24, RWE. Contract roles are disproportionately filled by referral.

## 30-Day Cadence

- **Week 1:** ship site/CV repositioning (done in this repo) + LinkedIn rewrite + recommendation requests; build the 30-lead shortlist → done, see `leads-shortlist.md` (the working tracker).
- **Weeks 2–3:** 5 outbound touches per day (mix recruiter / direct / board / warm). Log every reply. Run the pre-interview brief template (see `interview-narrative.md`) before every call.
- **Week 4:** convert interviews. "Available immediately" is closing leverage, not desperation — anchor the rate band before the call, per the playbook rule.

Funnel target (unchanged from playbook): 30 qualified leads → 20 direct applications/notes → 5 replies → 3 interviews → 1 long-term B2B contract.

## Sell-Factor Increases (Ranked)

1. **LinkedIn recommendations / referenceable colleagues** — currently missing entirely; costs one message each.
2. **Bitpanda case study as a sendable URL** — shipped with this change: `/projects/bitpanda-custody`.
3. **One technical write-up** (e.g. "Moving 45-minute exports to 12 with Node worker pools") — makes the RWE metric feel verifiable and gives outbound messages a hook. Optional; only if weeks 2–3 have slack.
4. **Verify demo repos** (`immobile-search`, `energy-reporter`) are public and presentable — a broken proof link is worse than none.

- Did 25+ outbound touches go out this week?

- Is any interview feedback repeating? Fold it into `interview-narrative.md` the same day.
- Are the recommendations in yet? Chase politely once.
